## Meetings
- Team: once a week for 30 minutes.
- Advisor: at least every two weeks.

## Working together
- All work lives in one shared project folder online, and each of us reviews the other's work.
- Every deliverable has one owner and an internal due date at least two days before the course deadline.
- If someone will miss a deadline, they tell the other at least 24 hours ahead.
- Decisions, requirements, estimates and tests are our own work. AI tools may help draft, and we say when we used them.

## Disagreements
1. Talk it through within 48 hours.
2. If still stuck, try both ideas on a small test.
3. If still stuck, the advisor decides.

## Changes
Both of us must agree to any change, and we tell the advisor.
